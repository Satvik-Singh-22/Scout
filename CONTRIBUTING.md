# Contributing to SCOUT

We welcome contributions to SCOUT! 

## Licensing Compliance

All contributions to this project must be licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under its Apache License 2.0 terms.

## DCO Sign-Off
To enforce contributor compliance and confirm that you have the right to submit your code, we require all contributors to sign off on their commits.

Please use the `-s` or `--signoff` flag when committing your changes:

```bash
git commit -s -m "Your commit message"
```

This adds a sign-off line at the end of your commit message, certifying that you have the rights to submit the work under the project's license.
